About the game

Blunder Construction is a chaotic physics-based roguelike deckbuilding blockstacker. You're a wizard responsible for building increasingly splendid luxury homes.

Each run is a series of building contracts. Levitate blocks into place with your magic wand and stack them into a tower that meets the contract's score quota. Once the quota is met, you can pull the lever to hand the building in, or keep stacking and push your luck: better buildings earn bigger rewards, but build too greedily and the whole thing comes down.

Between contracts you pick and buy rewards to grow your deck of Blocks and Magicks, then take on harder contracts with stranger modifiers: lopsided cliffs, volcano lairs, haunted graveyards. The run ends with a contract that tests every skill and synergy you've collected along the way.

About the developer

Robbobin is a solo developer. Blunder Construction has been in development since July 2026, originally starting as an entry for GMTK Game Jam 2026, and is Robbobin's first solo commercial release. No generative AI is used for any of its art or sound.
