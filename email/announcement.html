I'm Robbobin, a solo developer in the UK. I've just announced my new game, Blunder Construction. It's a chaotic blockstacking roguelike deckbuilder I'm hoping to release in Q3 2027. There's nothing quite like it yet, but I think the best comparison would be Tricky Towers meets Balatro.

Trailer: Watch on YouTube
Steam page: Blunder Construction on Steam

Release: Q3 2027
Platform: Steam

The King has sent you on a quest to become the greatest builder, stacking increasingly splendid luxury homes for his subjects in progressively stranger locations. You'll fill your deck with powerful / weird / questionable blocks and learn powerful magicks to meet their ever-growing desires for better-scoring buildings.

At the end of your run, you'll have to build the King your magnum opus, as a final test of your deckbuilding & blockstacking skills.
